How to use this

  • Notes — the whole chapter written out, with the diagrams from the book and worked answers. Read it before you revise, and come back to it whenever a flashcard catches you out.
  • Flashcards — every card is one question. Press space to show the answer, then g if you got it or a to see it again later. Use the filters at the top to drill one question type or one section at a time.
  • Nothing is saved to a server, so your progress stays on the device you are using. Finish a session before you close the tab.
